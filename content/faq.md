## What is Reminders+?

Reminders+ is a modern task and routine app designed to help you build consistency. Instead of overwhelming to-do lists, it focuses on one simple principle: complete at least one task every day and keep your streak alive.

## How does the streak system work?

Every day you complete at least one task, your streak increases by one. Miss a day, and it resets to zero. This simple mechanism motivates you to stay productive and build lasting habits through consistent daily progress.

## Is my data private?

Yes. Your tasks, projects and routines are stored on your device. There is no account, no tracking and no ads. If you turn on iCloud Sync, your data is synced through your own iCloud account via Apple. You can export all your data as a JSON file at any time.

## What platforms is it available on?

Reminders+ is available for iPhone and iPad (iOS 16.4 or later). Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your streak and today's tasks at a glance.

## Is Reminders+ free?

Yes, Reminders+ is free to download and use, including daily streaks, routines, widgets and data export. The optional Plus upgrade, available as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase through the App Store, adds AI Assist, more than 4 projects and iCloud Sync across your Apple devices.

## What is AI Assist?

AI Assist helps you create, find and organize tasks and projects in plain language. It runs on Apple's on-device Foundation Models, so your requests are processed on your device. It requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence.

## Can I import from Apple Reminders or Calendar?

Yes. With your permission, Reminders+ can import reminders from Apple Reminders and events from Apple Calendar into your tasks.

## Does it support multiple languages?

Yes! Reminders+ is available in 25 languages, including English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Chinese and Arabic.
